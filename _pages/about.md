Hi! I am a PHD in **Computer Science**, with research interests in **computer networks**, **programmable switches**, and **data center networking**.

My current research focuses on designing **high-performance and scalable network systems** with programmable data planes. In particular, I am interested in **packet classification**, **high-speed packet processing**, and **network acceleration on programmable switches**.

I also have broader research interests in **KV-Cache**, **Database**, and network resource optimization.

My research aims to explore how programmable network devices can be leveraged to improve the **performance, scalability, and efficiency** of modern network systems.

## Research Interests

- Programmable Data Planes
- P4 and Programmable Switches
- Data Center Networking
- High-Speed Packet Processing
- Packet Classification
- TCAM/SRAM Optimization
- KV-Cache
- Database
